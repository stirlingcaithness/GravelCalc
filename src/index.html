How the calculations work

All values are converted to metres and kilograms internally. Length/width: km × 1000. Thickness: mm ÷ 1000, cm ÷ 100. Density: t/m³ × 1000. Then:

Volume (m³) = Length (m) × Width (m) × Thickness (m)
Weight (kg) = Volume (m³) × Density (kg/m³)
Weight (tonnes) = Weight (kg) ÷ 1000

Mode A How much gravel do I need?

Total = (Length × Width × Thickness × Density) × (1 + Waste% ÷ 100)

Mode B How far will my gravel go?

Usable kg = Available kg ÷ (1 + Waste% ÷ 100)
Length = (Usable kg ÷ Density) ÷ (Width × Thickness)
